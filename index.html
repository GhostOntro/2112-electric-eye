THE GALLERY

ABOUT

Science in its post-capitalist iteration, operates as a cold and detached automaton, cataloging us as a collection of cellular structures, a network of nerves and a computational immune system built around a skeleton, some muscles, and a blob of purple jelly, once related to stars.

We travel across a conveyor belt being feed trinkets of techno-nihilism.

The universe holds no meaning under the cynical scope of a depersonalized "matter of fact" biocracy that bottlenecks us into the mere negotiation of the details of our eventual decomposition.

Cosmic events are manifestations both on the macroscopic and on the microscopic scale. The fact that the Andromeda Galaxy, our closest galactic neighbor, is approaching us by about 300 kilometers per second, and will merge with our own Milky Way, is a projected event so far ahead, that it becomes a form of poetic mythos for us, absolutely comparable in terms of emotional response to attempting a (re)construction of your first ever memory, or guessing the furnishings of your old age.

Space is not science. Space is Poetry.
